🚨 FRAUD ALERT – PLEASE BE AWARE
🩸 Do NOT make advance payments or send money to unknown numbers ❌

If someone contacts you offering to donate blood and asks for money, like petrol charges, travel expenses, food expenses, or any other payment BEFORE donating, please DO NOT TRANSFER MONEY.

✅ Ask the donor to come directly to the hospital/blood bank.
✅ Tell them to complete the blood donation first, and then have good will 😊
🙏 A genuine voluntary blood donor donates to SAVE A LIFE — NOT TO MAKE MONEY.

🚨 REPORT FRAUD & SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITIES
If you come across anyone demanding money, making fraudulent claims, or misusing the name of Red Star Blood Donors Club in connection with blood donation, please report it to our organisation immediately. Police action will apply accordingly.
